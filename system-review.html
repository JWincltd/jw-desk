Orientation

What the system is

Each morning before the open, the system screens the whole US market, runs a fan-out of analyst agents, and publishes three cards to a private board — highest-conviction picks, breakout option plays, and breakdown option plays. Every recommendation is recorded the moment it's published and graded as real prices come in, so the board shows a genuine track record next to today's picks. This build added two things the desk never had: it now calibrates its own rules against history, and it works the whole weekend instead of sitting idle.

The goal, unchanged: identify winning moves before they happen — and keep an honest, automatic record of whether we actually did.

Reading the record

How performance is measured

R — the risk unit
+1R means a play made what we risked on it; −1R means it lost that. A positive average R is profitable over many trades — even with a sub-50% win rate, when the winners run bigger than the losers.
Simulated P&L
The track record is graded on real daily prices with deliberately conservative rules — any ambiguity grades against us. It is not live broker fills.
"Calibrating"
Conviction tiers (strong / tilt / lean) and hit rates read "calibrating" until enough plays have closed to be more than noise — roughly two more weeks.
Known limits

Blind spots — where the system can't see

Stated plainly, so the two of you can weigh them honestly.

  • Core

    No live options data.The desk names plays but can't see implied volatility, greeks, or the option's own price — a thin edge in the underlying can be eaten by the option's spread and time decay.

  • Core

    Selection edge is still unproven.The whole system rests on the picks being good; the live ledger is too young to have confirmed that yet. Everything else is machinery around that central bet.

  • Data

    Daily bars only — no true intraday.The fastest hold we can test or trade is one session; genuine same-day setups aren't visible.

  • Execution

    Simulated fills, not a broker.No real slippage, liquidity, or short-borrow checks — paper P&L can flatter a strategy that's hard to actually execute.

  • Scope

    The "book" is a watch-list, not a weighted portfolio.It's our theses plus recent plays, with no position sizes — so concentration reads as directional, not exact.

  • Maturity

    Conviction bands aren't calibrated yet.The tiers are placeholders until the record matures; for now, trust the numbers, not the labels.

  • Trust

    The written memos can overreach.The narrative summaries occasionally assert a figure the system never computed. The data tables are the record of truth; the prose is color.

  • Coverage

    Macro calendar is sparse.Only the monthly jobs report is automatic; Fed and CPI dates must be entered and verified to appear in the pre-brief.

  • Infra

    Single free-tier data feeds.Prices, news, and filings each come from one free source with rate limits — a single point of failure per feed.

  • Signal

    The regime signal is one crude line.A single on/off — market above or below its 200-day average — with no volatility, breadth, or credit nuance.

For discussion

Additions to make it more effective

A menu to talk through together — roughly highest-leverage first.

  • 01

    Live options data — chains, IV, greeks.Turns "name a play" into "price a play": size trades to real cost and stop treating spread and time decay as free. The single biggest upgrade.

  • 02

    A real position book with sizes.Actual weights unlock true portfolio P&L, position sizing, and exact concentration instead of a directional watch-list.

  • 03

    Paper-broker, then live-broker integration.Real fills validate — or expose — the simulator, and give an execution record we can trust.

  • 04

    Full-text filing & transcript reading.The deep-read lists filings and summarizes headlines today; reading the actual 10-Q and earnings call is the next level of edge.

  • 05

    A richer regime model.Fold in volatility, market breadth, and credit spreads for smarter, graduated gating rather than one on/off line.

  • 06

    One consolidated weekend digest.Merge the several Saturday/Sunday notifications into a single week-ahead briefing so nothing is lost in the volume.

  • 07

    Verified macro calendar auto-feed.Wire in a confirmed Fed / CPI schedule so the pre-brief flags every landmine, not just jobs day.

  • 08

    Guardrails on the written memos.Constrain the summaries to only cite numbers the system actually computed — closes the "overreach" blind spot.

Audit

Not yet optimized — fix / improve / decide

The current rough edges, with a severity read.

  • Decide

    The short gate currently hides every breakdown play.Because the market is risk-on today, no puts reach the board at all. Confirm that "no mechanical shorts in a bull tape" is the behavior we want.

  • Improve

    Notification volume.Five-plus separate weekend reports arrive as separate alerts; a single digest would cut the noise.

  • Fix

    Memo fabrication.The narrative memos have twice cited a figure we never computed — constraining the prompts is a small, high-value fix.

  • Improve

    Deep-read and news-watch overlap on per-name headlines.Both pull company news; de-duplicating keeps each report sharp.

  • Improve

    Portfolio risk uses an unweighted list.Adding real position weights turns "directional crowding" into exact concentration.

  • Improve

    Short tickers escape the news flag.Two-letter symbols (MO, PM) are skipped to avoid false matches; matching on company name would catch them.

  • Tune

    Backtest and lab re-download two years each run.Caching the history would cut runtime and API load.

  • Tune

    The automation runtime is a version behind.GitHub is deprecating the Node version the jobs use; a routine bump keeps them from breaking later.

  • Populate

    Macro calendar is empty beyond jobs day.Add verified Fed / CPI dates and the pre-brief lights up immediately.
